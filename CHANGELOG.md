# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Initial multi-state residential release.
- Includes tariff profiles for Duke Energy service areas in North Carolina,
  South Carolina, Ohio, Kentucky, Indiana, and Florida.
- Provides state, utility, bill-plan, and plan-specific rate configuration.
- Supports flat, multi-tier, seasonal, TOU, CPP, demand, EV, solar, and
  minimum-bill calculation models.
